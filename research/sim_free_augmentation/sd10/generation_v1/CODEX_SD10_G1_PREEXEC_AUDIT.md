## Specific implementation hazards and corrections

1. **Wrong dataset/encoding risk.** `sfa/data.py` defaults to real_10, and v3 preprocessing was written for episodes 0–9. A new pool-ID adapter with input hashes is necessary. Calling it unchanged on SD10/Random10 would be invalid. Do not change the old loader or old caches.
2. **Different targets under repairs.** The historical runner tries scales 1,0.7,0.49. This study must bypass its search loop and attempt scale1 only. A failed target cannot become an easier accepted target. The selected source/ranking is frozen before reconstruction/IK.
3. **Position/image edits without reaching actions.** Shared absolute-position/axis retargeting must apply the same initial SE(3) to can and giver reach/grasp/lift waypoints in every arm. Verify the requested transforms before IK and exported actions after IK; do not overwrite requested targets with achieved FK or reward an unchanged copied can.
4. **Invalid terminal labels.** Legacy `planner3.actions` emits invalid trailing lead rows. All sources use `[3,T−6)` with exact native-row/time maps and a retained complete handover plus ≥15-frame receiver tail. Padding NaNs, keeping RGB with mismatched labels, or silently deleting different amounts by condition is invalid.
5. **Source-relative acceptance bias.** Legacy verifier thresholds include source-relative collision/dynamics allowances. Primary G1-A uses registered common absolute bounds, not per-source extra slack. Report inherited source-relative scores only as diagnostics. This does not resurrect the unsuccessful Gate1R verifier as a research method.
6. **Self-confirming RGB/rigidity checks.** A can constructed from a gripper attachment can trivially pass attachment drift, and the same projected mesh cannot independently validate target pixels. Separate visible artifacts from algebraic geometry and retain all failures/UNKNOWN regions. Raw MP4s and independent annotations, not metadata alone, are required after execution.
7. **Runtime/caches.** Original10 has more historical assets. Rebuild the 23-source union under the same pool-keyed cold-cache rule and charge standalone costs. Native durations/pace differ and can affect timeout frequency; report per-frame as well as full-bundle cost.

## Numerical threshold validation (fixtures, not experimental results)

The model-free checks reproduce deterministic pairing and correct signs. With 100 target pairs:

- Ten dispersed SD-only successes and no control-only success produce a 10-point difference; paired bootstrap CI [4,16] points, spatial-block CI [2,18], exact McNemar p=0.001953125. The registered rule passes this **fixture**.
- Fifty-five SD-only versus 45 control-only successes have the same 10-point point estimate, but paired CI [−10,30], spatial-block CI [−28,46], p≈0.3682. It correctly fails.
- Null/ceiling, reversed comparison, reproducibility, common-subset RGB statistics and nonfinite outcomes are checked. No target success probability or statistical power is inferred from these examples. Shared-source/local spatial dependence and the finite deterministic grid limit inference.

Ten additional usable targets out of 100 is a defensible practical screen. It does not guarantee sufficient precision. Do not weaken it or add targets after results. Both controls are mandatory. Geometry and RGB artifact uncertainty must remain separate.

A mechanism claim requires a distinct generator change and its own strong baselines.

No final generation result or learning/hardware/physical-force conclusion is established by this preregistration.
