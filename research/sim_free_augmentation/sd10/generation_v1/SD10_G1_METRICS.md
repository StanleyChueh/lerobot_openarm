# SD10-G1 frozen metrics and adjudication

2026-11-08; prospective, no generated results. Configuration numbers and this file jointly define the contract. Contradictions discovered before execution require a separately preserved amendment; never silently choose a favorable interpretation after seeing outputs.

## G1-A: complete geometric/label yield

Each of 300 primary attempt records has target ID, condition, locked source ID/ranking/score, shared config/target/code/model/input hashes, deterministic seed, source preprocessing status, stage timings, failure reason(s), planned target transforms, complete arrays or explicit missing fields. Preserve the failed input/partial outputs. An ordinary IK failure/timeout is Y=0, not missing data; an absent attempt manifest is study INCOMPLETE until accounted for. Exceptions must not disappear from denominators.

`Y_A=1` only when **all** following hold on the fixed `[3,T−6)` output interval. Assess full pre-export trajectory where necessary for donor/receiver transitions and action lead; no frame subsampling. Primary thresholds are absolute and identical across sources:

1. Arrays/state/action/object poses/timestamps are finite, correctly shaped, same row count, 30 fps, correct canonical source/native-row map. SE(3) rotations satisfy orthogonality/determinant tolerances 1e−6. At least 15 frames after donor release and all three command transitions are included. The full giver reach/lift, handover and receiver-carry segment is available; a grasp-only prefix fails.
2. Requested initial candidate lid is reached within **2 mm**, can axis within **1°**, under the registered estimated frame. Recompute from the exported object pose. Also report initial projected-lid error versus requested pixels, clearly self-consistency under an unvalidated camera.
3. Recompute FK from exported robot states versus **requested pre-IK TCP waypoints/trajectories**, not an actual-FK target overwritten after solving. Maximum IK residual ≤4 mm /2° at every requested frame; contact waypoints ≤2 mm /1°. Log all residuals, solved frame counts, seeds/iterations and failures, including frames where old code bypasses solving.
4. Robot states and commanded arm positions lie within URDF limits (1e−6 rad tolerance); IK clamping margin is 0.02 rad. State joint velocities at 30 fps do not exceed corresponding URDF velocities; acceleration ≤100 rad/s², consecutive arm-command jumps ≤0.25 rad. Log per-joint maxima and violating rows. No source-relative speed/limit relaxation for rough demonstrations. These bounds are declared kinematic screens, not validated actuator/dynamics limits.
5. Model-table clearance: lowest tilted cylinder rim no deeper than **10 mm** below candidate table z=0.09; distal robot links/grippers also no deeper than 10 mm. Exclude fixed base/proximal structures from the distal-table rule. All distal inter-arm sampled-hull penetration ≤5 mm; nonfinger-link/can-cylinder penetration ≤10 mm. Use full mesh convex hulls deterministically, all vertices plus the same seed-0 surface samples (up to 400/link), every frame. Finger/can overlap is reported separately as a grasp proxy. Geometry sampling can miss true collisions; no collision-free physical claim.
6. Steady holds: giver `[rc+12,lc−3)`, receiver `[ro+12,T−6)`. For each, compare exported `inv(T_TCP(t))*T_can(t)` to the source fitted attachment X for that hand; maximum translation ≤10 mm and full SO(3) error ≤5°. Report separate axis and yaw errors; cylinder yaw is model-internal, not independently observed. These checks may be satisfied by construction and are not independent corroboration of contact success.
7. Receiver capture: at the first measured-state closure `<0.7` after `lc`, require closure to occur in the retained handover; discrepancy between exported can and `T_L*X_L` ≤10 mm/5°. Record actual inferred closure/donor reopening and any negative dual-hold overlap; do not relabel commanded overlap as measured simultaneous contact or impose an invented force-contact interval. No closure before retained end → fail.
8. Object steps ≤80 mm /10° globally; at the table-to-giver and giver-to-receiver bookkeeping switches, ≤5 mm /3°. Retain/report the maximum switch errors even if a constructed attachment drift score is zero. Fixed initial can offsets, rotation/axis inconsistencies, moving can without a matching reaching/grasping hand, and wrong receiver attachment must be observable in these residuals.
9. Independently reconstruct every exported arm action from planned q, source lead and source residual; absolute discrepancy ≤1e−6 rad. Gripper commands equal the source ZOH stream and transitions exactly preserve rc/lc/ro under the native row map. Validate input hashes and per-joint columns: reproducing the source actions or an aggregate lead score is insufficient. No arm/gripper delayed-command corruption may pass simply because object rigidity is correct. Schema validity and deterministic label construction are engineering checks, not simulated or real execution.

Report: Y_A/100 by arm; every gate's failures/counts/severities; max/median/p95 residuals; elapsed time including rejects; selected source counts; source-to-target translation/rotation and pixel distance. Per-target target coverage is the accepted subset of the **same** 100 targets. Show its counts in 16 center/48 intermediate/36 edge cells and 54 near/46 far cells, the acceptance map, candidate-frame convex hull and nearest accepted-target distance. Do not quote coverage after moving failed targets or replacing them with easier poses.

All-target error summaries must retain missing/timeout values as unavailable plus failure counts; never average surviving residuals and call that unconditional quality. Accepted-only distributions are supplemental and explicitly selection-conditioned. Geometry inconsistency rate shares gates with Y_A and is not an independent replication of H1.

## G1-B: body-RGB artifact evidence

Render all complete finite plans within the common cap, including geometrically rejected ones. Define complete RGB R=1 only when an actual native-resolution MP4 exists, decodes fully, has the registered FPS/PTS/row count and matching state/action/frame hashes. A metadata entry or an identity copy without demonstrated target motion does not pass. Preserve diagnostic MP4s/frames for rejected plans; absent/unrenderable outputs have R=0.

Independent assessor(s), blinded to condition/source-assignment labels and numerical results, inspect **full clips**, then the same scripted windows around rc±6, lc±6, ro±6 and final 15 rows for every output. Assign five categories at clip level:

| Category | Required evidence |
|---|---|
| Incorrect copying | Can pixels/pose remain fixed or repeat the old appearance patch despite modeled relative motion; distinguish genuine nearly static geometry and symmetric texture. |
| Texture tearing | Material boundaries/label/lid seams split, stretch or duplicate inconsistently. |
| Ghost can pixels | A second can/residual identifiable can remains at the vacated source location. |
| Object/gripper occlusion contradiction | Gross visible depth ordering, interpenetration or missing-finger contradiction; do not use renderer z-buffer output as truth. |
| Temporal discontinuity | Can/robot appearance or relative position visibly pops/freezes without corresponding continuous source/trajectory motion. |

Labels: 0 none visible, 1 minor, 2 gross, UNKNOWN insufficient evidence. Record frame indices, artifact description and certainty. UNKNOWN is not a clean score. A bad raw source image and a newly introduced artifact should be distinguished through the same source-only side panel, after initial anonymous judgment; retain both labels. The first pass sees no condition name, accepted flag or source score. Save the presentation order/map separately and seal annotations **before unblinding**. Source visual identity may reveal condition membership; report suspected unblinding. Implementer self-ratings are not the primary independent annotations. A single independent assessor provides limited evidence; report inter-rater agreement/disagreements if a second assessor is available, never switch raters to rescue a result.

Gross contradiction G=1 if any category is 2. `clean_rgb=1` requires R=1, all five categories assessable and none gross; report minor artifacts too. Missing or UNKNOWN clips have clean_rgb=0 in all-100 yield. A clean **RGB/action bundle** additionally requires Y_A=1 and all labels/schemas valid. Failure fractions and unavailable assessment must be shown separately so that differing failure mechanisms remain visible.

Pixel provenance must be a **sidecar**, without changing rendered pixels per condition. Disjoint classes: unchanged source observation; warped source observation; observed background plate; synthesized/reflected/inpainted; UNKNOWN. Border reflection/extrapolation and unsupported holes count as synthesized or UNKNOWN, never observed support. Mixed pixels use the less-supported class. Report per-frame/phase whole-image fractions, edited-region fractions and independently indicated object-region fractions. Geometry-projected support is not independently validated visibility. If legacy rendering cannot supply trustworthy provenance, label those pixels UNKNOWN; do not invent measured inpaint percentages. Log empty masks, source fit/fallback flags, clipping/defocus and emitted layer/source-frame maps.

**Separate H2 rules:**

- Full-denominator clean_rgb and clean RGB/action bundle yield: paired arm differences over all100. A ≥10-point gain with paired-target and spatial-block CI lower >0 versus **both** controls supports better *usable artifact-screen yield*, which may be mediated by geometric feasibility.
- A claim of **lower visible artifact rate** also requires the common subset where all three arms have complete assessable RGB, ≥30 common targets, with ≥10-point lower gross-artifact prevalence versus each control and a paired-target CI lower >0 for the reduction. Freeze this subset mechanically after completeness/annotation, not by severity. Spatial-block sensitivity and all five category rates must also be reported. If insufficient common cases or pervasive UNKNOWN, H2 artifact-quality benefit is INCONCLUSIVE. This subset is outcome-conditioned and does not identify unconditional fidelity improvement.
- Never call a higher usable-bundle count proof of better pixels among comparable rendered outputs. No favorable subset can replace the all100 denominators.

Known ep6/frame91 copying and ep0/frame85 tearing in the older wrist renderer remain historical unresolved regressions; this **body-only** screen cannot pass/fix them. Source-only identity controls test plumbing and baseline source evidence, not target correctness. A later calibrated three-camera v4A comparison is still required. **Independently validated physical RGB fidelity is UNKNOWN** here: no real target views, reliable metric calibration or ground-truth texture are available. No force/grasp/friction/real-robot success is measured.

## Frozen statistics, uncertainty and stopping

The unit is target ID, paired across arms; never frames, joints, time samples or separate attempts. H1 uses Y_A; differences are **percentage points**, not relative percentages. Compare SD10−Original10 and SD10−Random10, retaining all100 outcomes. Reference implementation `paired_stats.py`: PCG64 seed20261108, 10,000 paired-target draws, percentile 2.5/97.5%; same target indices for both arms. The same routine also resamples 25 adjacent 2×2 spatial grid blocks and computes exact two-sided McNemar on discordant target outcomes.

H1/H3 meaningful geometric advantage: each difference ≥0.10 (at least ten additional complete targets), both target-CI lower bounds >0, both spatial-block-CI lower bounds >0 and both exact McNemar p≤0.05. Both comparisons must pass, an intersection claim; do not report only the easier control. At most this registered joint claim is confirmatory within the designed screen. H2 is a separate evidence family; its categories and remaining subgroup estimates are exploratory, not a menu for a replacement primary.

This is a **deterministic finite target grid**, not 100 random independent robot trials. Intervals are conditional target-distribution resampling diagnostics, not a probability guarantee for new scenes, physical poses, source sets or robots. Spatial correlation and shared selected-source artifacts matter; spatial blocks mitigate some local dependence but do not sample new sources. Include source-utilization and leave-one-assigned-source-out sensitivities; one random source set cannot establish curation superiority over the population of random tens.

Threshold validation before execution: with n=100 and paired outcomes d∈{−1,0,1}, 10 points is practically ten extra complete bundles but not necessarily statistically resolvable. Ten wins and no losses give exact McNemar p=0.001953125; 55 wins/45 losses give the same ten-point difference with much greater uncertainty and fail the zero-effect test. Thus discordance and clustering, not merely marginal rates, control precision. `audit_registration.py` checks these numerical fixtures, ceiling/null, condition sign and spatial pairing without generating trajectories. No power guarantee or invented outcome is claimed.

Geometry floor/ceiling, sparse RGB, wide CIs, or failed invariance = INCONCLUSIVE, with no threshold relaxation/extra target sampling. If a valid comparison excludes a ten-point advantage against either control, meaningful geometric source-set utility is unsupported; return to v4A/v4C unless the separately registered artifact evidence provides a specific reason to retain the branch. A positive geometry result can coexist with RGB failure and UNKNOWN physical accuracy. Final review must classify all of these separately.
