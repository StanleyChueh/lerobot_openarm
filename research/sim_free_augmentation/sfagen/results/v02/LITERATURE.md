# Literature refresh — 2026-11-13, abstract-level primary sources

- [DemoGen](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.16932): spatial demonstration adaptation motivates measured placement coverage.
- [RESTA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.18163), Oct 2026: temporal resampling motivates explicit clock accounting;
  not used to justify slowing the fixed-duration v02 episodes.
- [Augmentron](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09145), Nov 2026: geometry/multiview consistency is relevant to wrist ablation.
- [WARPED](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.10809), revised Oct 2026: wrist synthesis with Gaussian splats is related work;
  that renderer is not implemented here.

These papers do not establish this pipeline's RGB fidelity, contact validity or policy benefit. This bounded Step 2
retains the approved sfagen renderer and checks; no new learned generator or dependency is introduced.
