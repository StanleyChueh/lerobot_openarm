# Novelty check (literature as of November 2026)

**Our setting:** generate new bimanual demonstrations from about 10 real ones. The inputs are a single third-person
**RGB** camera (no depth) and the robot's URDF. No simulator and no generative model are used. Every episode must be
replayable on the real robot.

## What is NOT new in our pipeline (state it plainly in a paper)

- **Trajectories:** the object-centric segment transform is MimicGen / DemoGen. Moving a whole bimanual interaction rigidly is in the spirit of DexMimicGen's coordinated subtasks.
- **Robot rendering:** rendering the robot from its URDF with a calibrated camera is in Phantom / Masquerade. 3-D-consistent editing of real observations is in DemoGen, R2RGen and Real2Edit2Real.
- **Segmentation and calibration:** SAM2 masks and render-and-compare camera calibration are standard tools (CtRNet, RoboPose).

## Where a defensible contribution is

1. **Real pixels only, RGB only.** Every generated pixel is a real camera pixel moved by mesh-guided 3-D flow.
   - **Against generative and splat methods:** the closest RGB methods all add a generative or reconstructed appearance model (Real2Edit2Real: video model; R2RDreamer: I2V completion; ROPA: diffusion; RoboSplat: Gaussian splats).
   - **Against point-cloud methods:** DemoGen and R2RGen need depth and 3-D policies.
   - **What this gives:** our pipeline works with a single RGB camera and standard 2-D VLA policies (SmolVLA, GR00T, π0.5), with no learned appearance model and no hallucination.
2. **Measured image fidelity.**
   - **Benchmark:** a real-ground-truth pose-transfer benchmark (re-render real frame j+k from j), with a scoring region independent of the pipeline, held-out episodes and episode-bootstrap CIs.
   - **Decisions:** every pipeline choice (camera, masks, lag, renderer options) is accepted or rejected on it.
   - **Prior work:** the RGB-editing papers above report policy success but not per-frame fidelity against ground truth.
3. **Replay-safety by construction.**
   - **Checks:** every published episode passes explicit checks on the commanded action stream (limits, speed and acceleration envelopes of the real demos, mesh collision, hand distance, table, start pose, untouched parts), so it is directly replayable with `lerobot-replay`.
   - **Prior work:** most generators verify by simulation or not at all.
4. **One pipeline, several tasks, no per-task tuning.**
   - **Tasks:** a bimanual hand-over (Pringles) and a bimanual insertion (battery).
   - **Same everything:** generated by the same code and settings, with object models obtained without CAD and without labels (grasp-constrained render-and-compare; "what disappears when picked up" segmentation).
   - **Two variation families:** object position and interaction location (target position).
   - **Automatic calibration:** rig calibration (camera, table height, video/state lag) is automatic.

## Honest risks for a top venue

- **The components are known; novelty is in the combination and the evaluation protocol.** Reviewers will compare against R2RDreamer and Real2Edit2Real. Run at least one of them, or a "copy source frame" / homography baseline (we have the latter), on the same tasks.
- **Policy-level evidence is required.** Show real-only vs real + synthetic on held-out object and target positions on the real robot (SmolVLA, and GR00T or π0.5).
- **The limitations are real and must be shown.**
  - Fragmentation where the near-camera arm is re-posed.
  - Translation-only variation (≤ 6 cm).
  - Wrist cameras are not re-rendered.
  - No physics check.
- **One robot only.** Cross-robot generality is claimed by design (config-driven) but not yet demonstrated. A second robot dataset would strengthen it substantially.
