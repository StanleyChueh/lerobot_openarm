## Bottleneck (cProfile of one reconstruction, `simbench/results/r07/recon_profile_ep2.txt`, 311 s)
| Part | Time | Share |
|---|---|---|
| camera–joint time-offset search `_photometric_lag` (240 trial renders per demo) | 205 s | 66 % |
| background plates | 19 s | 6 % |

## Pitfalls seen in these sessions
- `pkill -f PATTERN` / `ps | grep | kill` inside a command whose text contains PATTERN kills your own shell (exit
  144). Collect PIDs into a file in one command, kill them in the next.
- Recon caches (`cache/recon_ep*.npz`, `sam2_*_ep*.npz`) do not encode the camera or flags beyond the cache name: after
  changing the camera or object settings, use a fresh work dir. `r06_setup.py` symlinks old recon caches — delete them
  when the camera changes.
- The SAM2 cache name does not include the lag; a lag change after the first `_masks` call reuses the old masks
  (pre-existing behaviour).
- 1000-frame AV1 episodes: decoding alone is minutes per run; reconstruction ≈ 6 min per demo per job.
